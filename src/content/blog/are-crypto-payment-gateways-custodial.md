**Yes, most crypto payment gateways are custodial.** In our September 2026 audit of 87 crypto payment gateways, **57 of 87 (66%) are custodial**: the provider's wallet receives the buyer's payment first, and you are paid out later, on the provider's schedule. Only **21 (24%) are non-custodial**, 4 are self-hosted, and 5 are hybrid.

Custody is the single most consequential property of a gateway, because every other risk follows from it. Fund freezes, payout delays, account closures and withdrawal fees can only happen when a third party is holding your revenue. A [non-custodial crypto payment gateway](/blog/non-custodial-crypto-payment-gateway) settles every payment straight to an address you control.

**Method:** classification of 87 gateways by their published custody model, from our [comparison of 87 crypto payment gateways](/blog/87-crypto-payment-gateways-compared).
