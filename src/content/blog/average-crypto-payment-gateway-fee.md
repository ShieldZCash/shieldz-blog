**The median crypto payment gateway fee is 1% per transaction.** Across 50 crypto payment gateways audited in August 2026, the median advertised processing rate is **1%**, with the field running from about 5% at the high end down to **0%**: **10 of the 50 charge no platform fee at all**.

A percentage fee scales with your revenue: 1% is 12 cents on a $12 sale but $100 on a $10,000 invoice, for identical work. That is the structural argument for flat-zero pricing. Watch for fees hidden on the exit, too: several "0%" gateways charge on payout or fiat conversion instead of on the payment.

**Method:** median of the standard advertised per-transaction rate for 50 gateways, from our [comparison of 50 crypto payment gateways](/blog/50-crypto-payment-gateways-compared).
