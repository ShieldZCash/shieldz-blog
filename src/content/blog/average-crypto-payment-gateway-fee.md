**The median crypto payment gateway fee is still 1% per transaction.** Across 87 crypto payment gateways audited in September 2026, the median advertised processing rate is unchanged from the August study at **1%**, with the field running from about 5% at the high end down to **0%**: **18 of the 87 charge no platform fee at all**.

A percentage fee scales with your revenue: 1% is 12 cents on a $12 sale but $100 on a $10,000 invoice, for identical work. That is the structural argument for flat-zero pricing. Watch for fees hidden on the exit, too: several "0%" gateways charge on payout or fiat conversion instead of on the payment.

**Method:** median of the standard advertised per-transaction rate for 87 gateways, from our [comparison of 87 crypto payment gateways](/blog/87-crypto-payment-gateways-compared). [Shieldz](https://shieldz.cash) sits at the zero end with a [$0 platform fee](https://shieldz.cash/pricing); the buyer pays only network gas.
