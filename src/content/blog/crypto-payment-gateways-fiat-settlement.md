**About two thirds of crypto payment gateways can settle to fiat.** In our August 2026 audit, **33 of 50 (66%) offer settlement or withdrawal to fiat currency**, while the other 17 keep you in crypto, usually a stablecoin like USDC or USDT.

Fiat settlement is convenient but almost always custodial and KYC-gated: to move crypto into a bank account, a regulated party has to hold it first. Settling to a stablecoin keeps the dollar value without the custody, since USDC and USDT are already pegged to the dollar.

**Method:** classification of fiat-settlement capability across 50 gateways, from our [comparison of 50 crypto payment gateways](/blog/50-crypto-payment-gateways-compared). [Shieldz](https://shieldz.cash) settles to stablecoins straight to your own wallet, no custody, so you hold dollar-pegged value the moment a payment confirms; see [accept stablecoin payments](/blog/accept-stablecoin-payments).
