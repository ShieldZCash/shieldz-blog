**Most crypto payment gateways require KYC, but not all.** Of 87 crypto payment gateways audited in September 2026, **53 (61%) require business verification** before you can accept a payment, 9 make KYC optional (usually above volume thresholds or for fiat), and **25 require no KYC at all**.

The pattern tracks custody: providers that hold funds and touch fiat are regulated like money institutions and must verify merchants, while providers that never hold the money have nothing to gate. Most of the 25 no-KYC gateways are non-custodial or self-hosted.

**Method:** classification of what each of 87 gateways requires from a new merchant, from our [comparison of 87 crypto payment gateways](/blog/87-crypto-payment-gateways-compared). [Shieldz](https://shieldz.cash) requires no KYC to start; you paste a wallet address and go. The trade-offs are in [accepting crypto payments without KYC](/blog/accept-crypto-payments-without-kyc).
