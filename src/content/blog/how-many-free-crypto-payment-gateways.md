**18 of 87 crypto payment gateways advertise a $0 platform fee.** In our September 2026 audit, eighteen gateways charge nothing on the payment itself, about the same 21% share as the August study, but the zeros are not all equal. The genuinely fee-free models are **self-hosted software** (run it yourself), **open protocols**, and **non-custodial gateways with no platform fee**. Others show 0% on acceptance but charge on the exit, on payout or on fiat conversion.

The rule when you see "free": check where the fee moved to. If it is not on the payment, look for it on the withdrawal or the conversion spread.

**Method:** count of gateways advertising a $0 platform processing fee among 87, from our [comparison of 87 crypto payment gateways](/blog/87-crypto-payment-gateways-compared).
