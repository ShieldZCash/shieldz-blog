**21 of 87 crypto payment gateways are non-custodial, about 24%.** In our September 2026 audit, **21 gateways settle every payment straight to a wallet you control** without ever holding a balance, plus 4 that are self-hosted software you run yourself. The remaining 57 are custodial and 5 are hybrid. The share has held steady at 24% since our August study of 50 gateways, even as the market grew: new entrants have skewed custodial too, not diluted it.

Non-custodial is the minority, which is exactly why it is the property worth checking first: it removes fund freezes, payout delays and withdrawal fees as a category, because there is no intermediary balance to freeze, delay or skim.

**Method:** custody classification of 87 gateways, from our [comparison of 87 crypto payment gateways](/blog/87-crypto-payment-gateways-compared). [Shieldz](https://shieldz.cash) is a hosted non-custodial gateway with a $0 platform fee, so you get the non-custodial model without running a server; read the [non-custodial gateway explainer](/blog/non-custodial-crypto-payment-gateway).
