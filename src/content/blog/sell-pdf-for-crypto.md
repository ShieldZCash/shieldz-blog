To sell a PDF for crypto you now need exactly three things: the file, a wallet address, and sixty seconds. Shieldz [pay-to-unlock](https://shieldz.cash/tools/pay-to-unlock) just gained direct file delivery: upload a PDF, Word document, spreadsheet, zip, ebook, image, or audio file up to **10 MB**, set a price, and get a hosted paywall link. The buyer pays crypto straight to your wallet and the download unlocks on their paid confirmation, automatically. No store, no signup, no KYC, and a $0 platform fee.

This closes the gap we watched real sellers hit. One seller listed a technical essay behind an unlock and had to end the excerpt with "the rest will be emailed after your tx confirms", because the old text-only payload could not carry a whole article. Manual delivery is a support queue pretending to be a checkout, so we removed the reason for it: files are delivered by the paywall itself, and text payloads now stretch to **100,000 characters**, enough for a full essay, a book chapter, or a long license file.

## Why not just use Gumroad?

Platforms like Gumroad, Payhip, or Etsy are fine products, but they all take a percentage of every sale and require an account on both ends of the trade. On small-ticket digital goods the percentage bites hardest, and payouts arrive on the platform's schedule, not yours.

A crypto paywall flips both properties: the payment settles [directly to your own wallet](/blog/non-custodial-crypto-payment-gateway) the moment it confirms, and the buyer needs no account anywhere, just a wallet with USDC, USDT, or almost any coin they already hold. There are also no chargebacks: a confirmed payment is final, which matters enormously for digital goods where the buyer keeps the file either way.

## How to sell a file for crypto, step by step

1. **Open the generator.** Go to [shieldz.cash/tools/pay-to-unlock](https://shieldz.cash/tools/pay-to-unlock). No signup.
2. **Upload and price it.** Paste the wallet address where money should land, attach your file (PDF, docx, xlsx, pptx, zip, epub, images, audio, video; up to 10 MB), optionally add teaser text or a license key alongside it, and set a price from $1 to $100,000. Confirm the acceptable-use policy.
3. **Share the link.** You get a hosted paywall showing the title, your pitch, and "Delivers: your-file.pdf". Post it in a bio, a DM, a tweet, a README. When a buyer pays, their confirmation page shows a download button; the file is never accessible before payment.

Add your email in the form and you also get a backup of your manage link plus a one-time email when your first sale lands. Selling from a dashboard instead? The same file upload is in [merchant.shieldz.cash](https://merchant.shieldz.cash) under Pay-to-unlock.

## What you can deliver

- **Documents**: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON
- **Bundles**: zip archives, epub ebooks
- **Media**: images (PNG/JPG/WebP/GIF), audio (MP3/WAV), video (MP4/MOV)
- **Text secrets** up to 100,000 characters: license keys, download links, full articles, private instructions; alone or alongside a file

Executable and web-page formats are deliberately not accepted, and downloads are always served as attachments, so a purchased file can never run as a page in the buyer's browser. Every unlock requires the acceptable-use confirmation (no adult, illegal, or non-consensual content) and settlement addresses are OFAC-screened.

## The delivery is trustless in both directions

The file lives behind the paywall, revealed only against a paid invoice on the buyer's own confirmation page. The money never touches Shieldz: the buyer pays your address directly, which you can [verify yourself](https://shieldz.cash/verify). Neither side has to trust the other to go first, and neither has to trust us with funds. Even AI agents can run this whole flow: an agent can [create and sell unlocks over MCP](/blog/ai-agents-sell-digital-products) with no API key.

## FAQ

**What file types can I sell?**
PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, zip, epub, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, images, audio, and video, up to 10 MB per file. Executables and web pages are not accepted.

**When does the buyer get the file?**
On their paid confirmation, after the payment settles on-chain to your wallet. The download link works only for a paid invoice; before payment it does not exist.

**What does it cost to sell a PDF for crypto?**
A $0 platform fee. The buyer pays their network gas (about a cent on Base). A $12 sale puts $12.00 of USDC in your wallet.

**Do I or the buyer need an account?**
No. You paste a wallet address; the buyer pays from any wallet or exchange. Add an email if you want sale notifications and a claimable dashboard later.

**What about files larger than 10 MB?**
Put the file on any storage you control and sell the link as the text payload; it stays hidden until payment exactly like a file. Larger native uploads are on our list.
